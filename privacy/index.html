Privacy · Last updated July 25, 2026

Privacy and analytics choices.

This portfolio uses privacy-first, consent-based analytics. Google Analytics does not load unless you explicitly accept it.

Collection

What is measured after consent.

With your permission, Google Analytics 4 measures page views, scroll and engagement activity, outbound link clicks, case-study selections, use of the portfolio print control, and clicks on the email contact link. Email addresses, names, message contents, and URL query strings are not sent as custom event data.

Analytics may process technical information such as browser and device type, approximate location, referring page, and the pages visited. This information is used only to understand portfolio performance and improve the usefulness of its content.

Consent

You control whether analytics loads.

Before a choice is made, no request is sent to Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager. Your choice is stored in your browser under portfolio-analytics-consent so it can be respected on later visits.

If you accept, Google Analytics may set first-party cookies beginning with _ga. If you decline, analytics remains unloaded. Withdrawing previously granted consent stops analytics on the next page load and removes those first-party analytics cookies where the browser permits it.

Configuration

Limited to portfolio measurement.

Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled. Analytics is not used for advertising, remarketing, ecommerce, account profiling, or cross-domain tracking. Event-level data retention is set to 14 months.

Analytics information is processed by Google under its privacy policy. This portfolio’s Search Console data concerns how pages appear in Google Search and is not used to track individual on-site behavior.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests.

For questions about this notice or requests concerning portfolio analytics, email semerci@gmail.com.
